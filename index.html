A studio for people who care about the details of a space.

ENSPACE Design Studio works across residential, commercial, office and retail projects. We plan layouts, choose finishes and fixtures, source furniture and lighting, and see each design through construction and installation.

Our approach stays close to the brief — the way you move through a home, the way a brand shows up in a retail space, the way a team works in an office. The design follows from that, not the other way round.
